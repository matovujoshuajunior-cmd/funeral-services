Our story

It began with one family, one loss, and one promise.

In 1999, our founder Daniel Century lost his mother unexpectedly. The funeral home his family used was efficient — and utterly cold. Nobody asked who she was. Nobody asked what she loved.

He promised himself that no family would ever feel that alone again. Century Funeral Services opened two years later in a small house on Willow Lane with one hearse, one chapel and a very stubborn belief: that a funeral should feel like the person it honours.

Twenty-five years later we have cared for more than four thousand families. The house on Willow Lane is still our home, the chapel still has the same wooden floors, and the promise has never changed.
