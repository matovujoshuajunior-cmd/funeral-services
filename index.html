About Relieve Funerals

We are a family-owned funeral service provider dedicated to offering compassionate, dignified, and personalized care to families in Kampala and beyond.

Our Story

Relieve Funerals was founded in 2012 with a simple yet profound mission: to provide families with compassionate, professional, and dignified funeral services. Located on Kafeero Street in Mulago, Kampala, we have grown to become a trusted name in the community.

We understand that the loss of a loved one is one of life's most challenging experiences. Our team is committed to easing the burden by handling every detail with care, respect, and transparency.

We honor all traditions, cultures, and faiths, ensuring that each service is a beautiful reflection of the life lived.

Our Values

At the heart of Relieve Funerals are values that guide everything we do:

  • Compassion & Empathy
  • Integrity & Transparency
  • Professional Excellence
  • Cultural Respect
  • Personalized Care
  • Community Focus

We believe that every life is unique and deserves to be celebrated in a way that brings comfort and healing to those left behind.
